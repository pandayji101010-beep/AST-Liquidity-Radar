These are system design rules / specifications. Their live execution and campaign-wide risk enforcement must be implemented and validated separately before being treated as active trading controls.
BTC candles use Binance USD-M Futures history and live streams. GOLD candles use native Vantage MT5 XAUUSD broker OHLC candles when the bridge is connected. Direction changes require multi-timeframe reversal confirmation; it can lag and is not a guarantee. Reaction levels are candidate horizontal prices calculated from Binance candle history; they are not guaranteed reversal points. AST pressure and setup rule scores are rule-completion indices, not probabilities or guaranteed signals. Momentum continuation plans remain available in the Trade Level Board after confirmation; breakout watches are not entry instructions. Binance depth only shows visible orders on that venue; orders can be cancelled. Liquidation stream is a throttled snapshot feed, not a complete market-wide liquidation ledger. BTC Vantage quotes are mapped using a rolling median ratio; GOLD uses direct Vantage MT5 broker candles.
