Direction changes require multi-timeframe reversal confirmation; it can lag and is not a guarantee. Reaction levels are candidate horizontal prices calculated from Binance candle history; they are not guaranteed reversal points. AST pressure and setup rule scores are rule-completion indices, not probabilities or guaranteed signals. Momentum continuation plans remain available in the Trade Level Board after confirmation; breakout watches are not entry instructions. Binance depth only shows visible orders on that venue; orders can be cancelled. Liquidation stream is a throttled snapshot feed, not a complete market-wide liquidation ledger. Price-action trade cards combine confirmed liquidity-reversal and momentum-continuation setups across all existing timeframes, with retest entry zones, structural stops, and fixed 1.5R / 2R planning targets. They are educational levels, not guaranteed fills or price destinations; account for spread, slippage and risk.
